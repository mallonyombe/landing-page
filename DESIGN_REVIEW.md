# EL-ROI’S SPA — v4 design review

## Direction
V4 is not a redesign of the v3 concept. It is a controlled refinement of the same photo-led editorial direction.

## Experience architecture
1. Arrival — cinematic hero and one clear action.
2. Orientation — concise statement of what the spa offers.
3. Discovery — horizontal service index that follows scroll position.
4. Experience — one visual chapter per service family.
5. Care — safety/comfort guidance without interrupting the visual rhythm.
6. Visit — hours, location and directions.
7. Conversion — WhatsApp is the primary action and remains available on mobile.

## Why this direction
Current beauty/spa design references consistently emphasize strong photography, mobile-first browsing, clear booking paths, generous spacing and editorial typography. The design avoids the common service-card grid and instead gives each treatment family enough visual space to communicate atmosphere.

## What was deliberately not added
- account system
- booking database
- chatbot
- AI features
- dashboard
- unnecessary pages
- decorative animation without a functional purpose

The client brief is a QR-driven digital menu, not a full spa management platform.

## Demo-data policy
All operational values added in v4 — phone, address, prices and durations — are clearly demo values and must be replaced with client-confirmed information before public launch.
